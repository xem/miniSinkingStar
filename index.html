Demo:

- You are the red square
- Use Arrow keys to play and R to reset
- You need to reach the blue exit
- You can push the grey blocks
- Activate yellow buttons to toggle orange obstacles
- Goblins (brown squares) kill dragons and goblins around them (not in diagonal)
- Dragons (brown triangles) kill creatures placed in front of them (only one per move)
- Pink obstacles disappear when all enemies are neutralized
