Rules (spoilers)

- You are the hero (red square)
- You need to reach the exit (blue square)
- You can push the crystals (grey squares) and the enemies (brown)
- Activate yellow buttons to toggle orange obstacles
- Goblins (brown squares) kill creatures around them (not in diagonal)
- Dragons (brown triangles) kill creatures placed in front of them (only one per move)
- Pink obstacles disappear when all enemies are neutralized
- All moving objects can be destroyed by a reappearing orange obstacle
