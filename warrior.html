MiniSinkingStar

Tiny "Sinking Star Hes Wizard" implementation in HTML+JS.
Size: 701b zipped (engine only) / 1.1kb (full demo).
Play with arrow keys / Z to undo / R to reset.

Rules

- You are the red square
- You need to reach the blue exit
- You can push the grey blocks (even many at once)
- Activate yellow buttons to toggle orange obstacles
- Goblins (brown squares) kill creatures around them (not in diagonal)
- Dragons (brown triangles) kill creatures placed in front of them (only one per move)
- Pink obstacles disappear when all enemies are neutralized
