MiniSinkingStar - Warrior

Tiny "Sinking Star Wizard" implementation in HTML+JS
Game engine: 925b (508b gzipped) / Full demo: 1858b (1023b gzipped)
Play with arrow keys / Z to undo / R to reset / L to load a custom level

Rules (spoilers)

- You are the hero
- You need to reach the exit (green)
- You can push the crystals and the monsters
- Goblins kill creatures around them (not in diagonal)
- Dragons kill creatures placed in front of them (once per hero step)
- Yellow buttons toggle orange obstacles (they can destroy objects)
- Purple obstacles disappear when all the monsters are neutralized
